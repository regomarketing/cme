Toolholding Demo

The CME Demo Series brings the shop floor to your screen. Each video is filmed live at the Center for Machining Excellence in Whitestown, Indiana — a 50,000+ sq ft facility built by REGO-FIX USA to give machinists, engineers, and shop owners hands-on access to the industry's best equipment, tooling, and technology.

This demo showcases REGO-FIX powRgrip® toolholding in action on a live CNC setup. You'll see how powRgrip delivers industry-leading runout accuracy and clamping force without the heat required by shrink-fit systems — and why it's the preferred toolholding solution in high-precision environments worldwide.

The CME Demo Series is produced in partnership with REGO-FIX USA and the CME's network of participating technology partners. New demos are added regularly across categories including workholding, CAD/CAM, automation, cutting tools, and more. All demos are free to watch.
