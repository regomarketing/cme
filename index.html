REGO-FIX powRgrip®
Toolholding Demo

Center for Machining Excellence Whitestown, IN
Share

The CME Spotlight Series brings the shop floor to your screen. Each video is filmed live at the Center for Machining Excellence in Whitestown, Indiana — a 50,000+ sq ft facility built by REGO-FIX USA to give machinists, engineers, and shop owners hands-on access to the industry's best equipment, tooling, and technology.

This spotlight showcases REGO-FIX powRgrip® toolholding in action on a live CNC setup. You'll see how powRgrip delivers industry-leading runout accuracy and clamping force without the heat required by shrink-fit systems — and why it's the preferred toolholding solution in high-precision environments worldwide.

The CME Spotlight Series is produced in partnership with REGO-FIX USA and the CME's network of participating technology partners. New spotlights are added regularly across categories including workholding, CAD/CAM, automation, cutting tools, and more. All spotlights are free to watch.
